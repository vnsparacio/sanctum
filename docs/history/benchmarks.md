# Benchmark history

The dated source reports compared local 4B and private 80B across 203 blinded qualitative cases. After unblinding: 80B won 108 (53.2%), 4B won 47 (23.2%), with 48 ties. Among decisive cases, 80B won 69.7%. Objective scores were 25/32 (78.1%) for 80B and 21/32 (65.6%) for 4B.

The strongest 80B gains were accuracy, helpfulness and calibration, particularly structured/technical work. Local 4B remained useful for routine/tool work and some tone/writing categories. Earlier hosted 30B text results were insufficiently differentiated; hosted 235B offered stronger nuanced judgment. A later 30B visual tier serves a different capability.

A 54-pair generic prompting experiment produced limited rescues and regressions. Learned semantic routing also failed fresh/grouped holdouts after strong small-set results. Neither model size nor classifier accuracy became an authorization boundary.

Both 4B and 80B failed some policy and deterministic long-context cases. These are historical aggregate results, not a new benchmark of this candidate, current provider availability or hardware purchase guidance. Raw personal benchmark prompts and qualitative corpora are excluded. Preserve blinded scoring, frozen data lineage and spent-holdout distinctions in future work.
